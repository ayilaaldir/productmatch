## Remaining boundaries

1. Excel 365 desktop is the intended primary platform; verify native UI behavior on the intended installation before a client demonstration. Google Sheets conversion is explicitly untested.
2. The dataset and trading outcomes are simulated. Real model facts are labeled separately. Manufacturer identifiers, source URLs and research access dates are not fabricated.
3. Fuzzy scores are external snapshots. Python refresh is required for changed/appended records. Reordering MASTER RAW can change the earliest-row duplicate survivor; refresh fuzzy after that operation even if signatures still show Current.
4. CSV exports read saved caches and require calculation and saving first. They do not update automatically with the workbook.
5. Capacity is 300 master / 350 supplier / 100 research records. Only Specification research applies automatically. Other attributes stay in the evidence queue.
6. Suspected duplicate detection requires complete brand/model/variant identity; incomplete records can evade that flag. Manual decisions are current-state inputs rather than a complete automatic historical audit log.
7. SanDisk and Crucial evidence includes official indexed content with direct-fetch caveats recorded in the research notes. Hardware-version and SDHC/SDXC contradictions remain unapplied.

No time savings, production accuracy improvement, client engagement or financial impact is claimed.
